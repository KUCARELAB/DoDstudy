This data was collected by Dr. Kelsie Forbush as part of the federally funded Longitudinal Eating Disorder Assessment Project (LEAP). This work was supported by The Assistant Secretary of Defense for Health Affairs endorsed by the Department of Defense, in the amount of $1,721,698 dollars through the Peer Reviewed Medical Research Program under Award Number W81XWH1910207. The purpose of the study was to develop and test a military-specific transdiagnostic eating disorder survey and screening tool in a nationally representative sample of veterans. We collected information on eating disorder pathology, comorbid mental health conditions, etc., through validated assessment tools.
The raw data were de-identified by removing identifiable variables, including names, dates of birth, and height, and recoding certain responses into broader categories.
The dataset is provided in SPSS format (.sav), which preserves variable and value labels.
